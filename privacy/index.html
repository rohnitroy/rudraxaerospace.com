01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how RudraX Aerospace ("RudraX", "we", "us") handles information when you visit rudraxaerospace.com (the "Website").

The Website is informational. It presents our company, research, documentation and general knowledge about our work. It has no user accounts, sign-up forms, contact forms, newsletters, payments or comment features.

02Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address through the Website.

03Technical data

Like every website, the Website has to be delivered to your browser by servers. When it is, some technical information is processed automatically by our hosting provider, such as:

  • your IP address;
  • browser type and version, and operating system;
  • the page requested, and the date and time of the request.

This information is used only to deliver the Website, keep it secure and protect it from abuse. We do not use it to identify you, build profiles or track you across other websites.

04Cookies and analytics

The Website does not set cookies. It does not use advertising pixels, social media trackers or cross-site tracking.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not store your IP address. It records anonymous information such as the page viewed, the referring website, your country, and your device, browser and operating system type. Visitors are not identified and are not tracked across days or across other websites.

05How information is used

The limited technical data described above is used only to operate and secure the Website, and the anonymous analytics only to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we do not use it for marketing.

06Third-party services

We rely on a small number of service providers to run the Website:

  • Vercel Inc. hosts the Website, processes the technical data described in section 03 and provides the anonymous analytics described in section 04. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the Website. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. If the Website adds links to other websites in future, we are not responsible for how those websites handle your information.

07Children

The Website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

08Security

The Website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we do not collect personal information through the Website, we hold no user database that could be exposed.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since the Website does not collect personal information, we hold no personal data about visitors.

10Changes to this policy

If the Website changes, for example by adding a contact form or newsletter, we will update this policy before that feature goes live and change the "Last updated" date above.
